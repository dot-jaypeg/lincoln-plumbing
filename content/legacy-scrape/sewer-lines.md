## Signs Your Sewer Line Needs Attention
Sewer line problems rarely fix themselves — and they get more expensive the longer they're ignored.
01
### Frequent Backups
Multiple drains backing up at the same time, especially the lowest drain in the house.
02
### Sewage Odor Outside
A persistent sewage smell in your yard, often near where the line runs.
03
### Soggy Or Sunken Yard
Unexplained wet, soft, or sunken patches in your lawn above the sewer line.
04
### Slow Drains Everywhere
Every drain in the house is slow at once — a strong sign the main line is affected.
Your Options
## Trenchless vs. Traditional Repair
Depending on the damage, you may not need to dig up your entire yard to fix it.
### Traditional Dig-And-Replace
- Requires digging a trench along the damaged line
- Disrupts landscaping, driveways, or hardscape
- Often necessary for severely collapsed or misaligned pipe
- Typically longer job timeline
### Trenchless Repair
- Repairs or replaces the line with minimal digging
- Preserves your yard, driveway, and landscaping
- Often faster to complete
- Great fit for cracks, root intrusion, and pipe lining
