## Signs You Need Hydrojetting
Regular snaking only breaks a hole through a clog — hydrojetting clears the entire line. Here's when it's the right call.
01
### Recurring Clogs
The same drain keeps clogging no matter how many times it's been snaked.
02
### Tree Root Intrusion
Roots have grown into your sewer line and are restricting or blocking flow.
03
### Grease Buildup
Years of grease and buildup have narrowed your pipes from the inside out.
04
### Multiple Slow Drains
Several drains in your home are slow at the same time — often a main line issue.
The Difference
## Snaking vs. Hydrojetting
Both clear clogs — but only one clears the whole pipe.
### Snaking
- Punches a hole through the clog
- Leaves buildup on the pipe walls
- Clogs often return within weeks or months
- Good for a quick, minor blockage
### Hydrojetting
- Scours the entire interior of the pipe
- Removes grease, scale, and root intrusion completely
- Longer-lasting results, fewer repeat calls
- Ideal for recurring or severe clogs
