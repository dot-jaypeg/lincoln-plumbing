## Signs Your Water Heater Needs Attention
Most water heater failures show warning signs first — catching them early can save you a cold shower and a bigger bill.
01
### No Hot Water
Water isn't heating at all, or runs out much faster than it used to.
02
### Rusty Or Cloudy Water
Discolored hot water is often a sign of a corroding tank from the inside.
03
### Strange Noises
Rumbling, popping, or banging sounds coming from the tank.
04
### Leaking Around The Tank
Any pooling water or moisture near the base of the water heater.

## Traditional Tank vs. Tankless
We'll help you pick the right fit for your home and budget — no upselling, just the facts.
### Traditional Tank
- Lower upfront installation cost
- Stores and continuously heats a set amount of water
- Simple, well-understood technology
- Hot water can run out with heavy use
### Tankless
- Heats water on demand — no storage tank needed
- Continuous hot water, no running out
- More energy efficient over time
- Compact footprint, longer typical lifespan
