## Signs Your Garbage Disposal Needs Attention
A struggling disposal usually gives warning signs before it fails completely.
01
### Won't Turn On
No sound at all when you flip the switch — could be electrical or a jammed motor.
02
### Humming, Not Spinning
The unit hums but the blades won't turn — usually a jam or worn motor.
03
### Leaking Under The Sink
Water pooling below the disposal, often from a cracked housing or bad seal.
04
### Frequent Jamming
Needing to reset the unit constantly, even with normal use.
Honest Advice
## Repair Or Replace?
We'll tell you honestly which one makes sense for your situation — not just push a new unit.
### Repair Might Make Sense If...
- The unit is relatively new and otherwise in good shape
- The issue is a simple jam, reset, or minor part failure
- You want the lowest-cost fix available
### Replacement Might Make Sense If...
- The unit is old or has failed multiple times before
- There's a leak from a cracked housing
- You want a quieter or more powerful unit
