Accessibility

How Lincoln Plumbing & Rooter makes this website usable for everyone, and how to get help.

Call 909-780-0887 Get A Free Quote

Lincoln Plumbing & Rooter, INC wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control, magnification or captions.

Our Standard

We're working to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, and we check the site with automated tools and by hand.

What We've Done

  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Keyboard access to menus, links and forms
  • Labeled form fields with clear error messages
  • Pages that work when zoomed and on small screens

Known Limitations

  • The homepage shows a short, silent background video that loops and has no pause control. It's decorative and carries no information.
  • Some pages include content from other companies that we don't control, such as Google Maps, Yelp review widgets and our financing partner's application.

Need Help or Found a Barrier?

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you'd like information in a different format, contact us and we'll help:

  • Phone: 909-780-0887
  • Email: contact@lincolnp.com

Please tell us the page and what went wrong. We aim to respond within 2 business days. You can always call us to request a quote or service instead of using an online form.
