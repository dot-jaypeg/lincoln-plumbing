Common Issues Facing Commercial Kitchens

Restaurants confront plumbing problems that demand expertise and quick solutions. At Lincoln Plumbing & Rooter, we know how to handle the issues unique to commercial kitchens:

  • Grease accumulation slowing drainage and risking clogs
  • Hidden leaks under sinks or inside walls threatening structural integrity
  • Malfunctioning dishwashers, ice makers, and beverage systems that disrupt service

Our goal is to identify these problems promptly, fix them efficiently, and safeguard your restaurant’s reputation for quality and cleanliness.
