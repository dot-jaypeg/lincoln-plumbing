Sound Familiar?

Signs You Have A Hidden Leak

Hidden leaks waste water and cause damage long before they become visible — catching them early saves money and prevents bigger repairs.

Unexplained High Bill

Your water bill jumps with no change in usage — a classic sign of a hidden leak.

Sound Of Running Water

You hear water running when every fixture in the house is off.

Damp Spots Or Mold

Unexplained damp patches on walls, ceilings, or floors, or musty odors.

Low Water Pressure

A sudden drop in water pressure that wasn't there before.

Why It Matters

Guessing vs. Professional Detection

Finding a leak the wrong way can cost you more than the leak itself.

Guessing & Digging

  • Opening walls or floors hoping to find the source
  • Risk of unnecessary demolition and repair costs
  • No guarantee the actual leak gets found
  • Can take multiple attempts to locate

Professional Leak Detection

  • Electronic listening equipment and moisture sensors pinpoint the source
  • Minimal to no unnecessary demolition
  • Confirms the exact location before any repair work starts
  • Faster, more accurate, and ultimately less costly
