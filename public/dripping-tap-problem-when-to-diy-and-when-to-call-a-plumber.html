Dripping Tap Problem? When to DIY and When to Call a Plumber

A dripping tap may seem like a minor annoyance, but it can waste a significant amount of water over time. According to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, household leaks account for more than 1 trillion gallons of wasted water annually. While some dripping tap issues are easy to fix on your own, others require the expertise of a professional plumber. Here’s how to determine the best course of action.

Potential Causes of a Dripping Tap Problem

A tap in good condition should never drip. If yours does, it’s important to understand the underlying cause before attempting a fix.

Worn-Out Parts

General wear and tear over time can cause components of your tap to fail. Common parts that may need replacing include:

  • Washers
  • O-rings
  • Jumper valves

Replacing these parts can often resolve a drip. For newer taps, such as those with ceramic disks, specialized parts may be required, and these can sometimes be harder to find. Regular maintenance can extend the life of your tap and prevent drips from occurring.

Valve or Pipe Damage

Damage to the valve or connected pipes is another common cause of dripping taps. This might happen due to:

  • A sudden change in water pressure.
  • Impact from physical damage, such as something hitting the tap.
  • Issues with the pipes, which could cause multiple taps to drip.

If you notice dripping from several taps in your home, the issue likely stems from the pipes and will require professional attention.

Fixing a Leaking Tap Yourself

For minor issues, such as replacing a worn washer, you may feel confident tackling the repair yourself. Before attempting a DIY fix, consider the following:

When DIY Repairs Are Appropriate

  • Worn Parts: If the problem is due to a common part, such as a washer or O-ring, replacing it is relatively straightforward.
  • Availability of Tools: Ensure you have the necessary tools to complete the repair. Most parts are available at local hardware stores.

Tips for DIY Repairs

  • Identify the make and model of your tap to ensure you purchase the correct replacement parts.
  • Turn off the water supply before starting any repair work.
  • Follow detailed instructions or tutorials to avoid mistakes that could lead to further damage.

If you lack confidence or experience, it’s best to hire a professional plumber to avoid causing more harm than good.

When to Call a Plumber

For more complex issues or widespread problems, hiring a professional plumber is highly recommended.

Signs You Need a Professional

  • Multiple Dripping Taps: This indicates a problem with the pipes, not just the individual taps.
  • Damaged Valves or Pipes: Repairs involving internal components or pipe damage require expertise.
  • Persistent Drips After DIY Fixes: If your efforts to stop the drip have failed, a plumber can assess the problem more thoroughly.

Benefits of Hiring a Plumber

  • Accurate Diagnosis: Professionals can identify the exact cause of the problem and recommend the best solution.
  • Proper Repairs: Plumbers have the tools and expertise to carry out repairs safely and effectively.
  • Insurance Protection: Licensed and insured plumbers provide peace of mind, covering unexpected damages during the repair process.

Preparing for a Plumber’s Visit

To ensure your plumber can work efficiently, take a few preparatory steps:

  • Clear the Area: Remove items from cupboards or counters near the affected tap to provide easy access.
  • Turn Off the Water: Ask your plumber if they’d like the water turned off in advance to save time.
  • Confirm the Quote: Request a detailed estimate that includes parts and labor to avoid surprise fees.

After the repair is completed, ask for tips on routine maintenance to prevent future issues.

Dealing With a Dripping Tap Problem

Dripping taps can be caused by worn parts, valve damage, or pipe issues. Small repairs may be manageable as a DIY project, but complex problems require the expertise of a professional plumber. Properly diagnosing and addressing the issue early can save you money and prevent water waste.
