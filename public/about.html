Our Story

Two Brothers, One Simple Mission

Honest, high-quality plumbing that customers can trust — that's the whole idea, and it hasn't changed since day one.

2021Company Founded

Lincoln Plumbing was founded by two brothers with a simple mission: to provide honest, high-quality plumbing services that customers can trust. What started as a small, family-run operation with just a truck, a handful of tools, and a big dream has grown into a well-respected name in the plumbing industry.

Our journey began with a commitment to hard work and a deep passion for helping people. From fixing leaky faucets to tackling major pipe replacements, we treated every job — big or small — with the same level of care and professionalism. That dedication quickly earned us the trust of homeowners and businesses throughout the community.

Over the years, Lincoln Plumbing has grown, but our values have never changed. We've built a reputation for reliability, transparency, and quality craftsmanship. With two decades of hands-on experience, our team combines traditional know-how with the latest tools and techniques to deliver solutions that last.

Thank you for trusting Lincoln Plumbing.
