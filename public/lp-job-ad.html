Straight From The Shop

A Growing Company Built By Plumbers

Lincoln Plumbing & Rooter was founded by two brothers with a simple mission: honest, high-quality plumbing work that customers can trust. That started with one truck and a handful of tools, and it's grown into a busy shop serving homes and businesses across San Bernardino and the Inland Empire.

More work means more trucks on the road, and we need good people in them. If you want to learn the trade the right way, with real equipment and a crew that has your back, we want to hear from you.
