How Expert Rooter Services Can Keep Your Drains Clear and Problem-Free

Clogged drains can quickly turn into serious plumbing problems if not handled properly. At Lincoln Plumbing & Rooter, our specialized rooter services tackle even the most stubborn clogs, helping you avoid potential property damage and keeping your plumbing system running smoothly. Read on to learn more about how professional rooter services work and why they’re an essential part of maintaining a healthy plumbing system.

A Full Range of Rooter Services for Any Blockage

Rooter services cover various methods designed to address a wide range of common plumbing issues. From routine maintenance to emergency blockages, our rooter solutions are tailored to meet your unique needs.

Mechanical Snaking

Mechanical snaking involves using a specialized tool to break up clogs caused by debris, grease, and other common obstructions. This method is particularly effective for clearing drains in sinks, showers, and toilets.

Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting is an advanced cleaning technique that uses high-pressure water to flush out grease, sediment, and other buildup in your pipes. It’s a highly effective preventive measure, keeping pipes clean and minimizing future clog risks.

Tree Root Removal

Tree roots are a frequent cause of sewer line blockages. When roots infiltrate pipes, they create severe blockages that can lead to serious plumbing issues. Our rooter services safely remove these roots, protecting your pipes and restoring proper drainage.

Common Issues Resolved with Rooter Services

Rooter services can tackle a variety of common issues that affect your plumbing system. Some frequent culprits include:

Grease Buildup

Grease from cooking and washing can accumulate in pipes over time, causing blockages. Hydro jetting is an effective solution for removing grease and preventing clogs.

Debris and Sediment Accumulation

Natural sediment and debris can accumulate in pipes, leading to blockages and sluggish water flow. Mechanical snaking and hydro jetting both provide solutions to clear this buildup.

Foreign Objects

Items accidentally flushed or washed down drains, like toys or food scraps, often cause blockages. Rooter services can safely remove these objects to restore normal water flow.
